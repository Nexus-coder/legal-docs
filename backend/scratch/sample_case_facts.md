# Sample Case Facts: Adverse Possession

**Matter**: John Mutua Kilonzo v Sarah Wanjiku Kamau
**Jurisdiction**: Environment and Land Court (ELC)
**Topic**: Adverse Possession

## Case Facts for Input

The Plaintiff, John Mutua Kilonzo (ID No. 12345678), has been in continuous, open, and notorious occupation of the parcel of land known as LR No. 1234/567 situated in Ruiru, Kiambu County, since the 12th of March, 2005. 

The property was originally registered in the name of the Defendant, Sarah Wanjiku Kamau, who relocated to the United Kingdom in 2004 and has not asserted any rights of ownership or visited the land for a period exceeding 18 years. During this time, the Plaintiff entered the land as a squatter and proceeded to construct a permanent three-bedroom residence and a perimeter stone fence.

The Plaintiff has also been paying the requisite land rates to the Kiambu County Government and has connected utility services, including water from the Ruiru-Juja Water and Sewerage Company (RUJWASCO) and electricity through account number 9876543-01 in his own name. 

At no point during the period between 2005 and 2024 did the Plaintiff seek permission from Sarah Wanjiku Kamau, nor did the Defendant or her agents, such as her brother Peter Kamau, attempt to evict the Plaintiff. Consequently, the Plaintiff seeks a declaration that the Defendant's title to the suit property has been extinguished by the operation of the Limitation of Actions Act (Cap 22) and that the Plaintiff has become the absolute owner by way of adverse possession.
